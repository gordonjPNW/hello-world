# Phase 8 — Validation

**Time:** 30 minutes, one drive.

Prove it works before handing the keys back. Do this in the car, engine running, not from memory.

## The record

Fill this in and keep it with the car's paperwork. Next time something acts up, this is the baseline
that tells you whether it's new.

```
Vehicle:            2017 Chevrolet Cruze hatchback LT
VIN:                ______________________
Radio RPO:          IOB / IO5 / IO6
Screen:             7" / 8"
Embedded nav:       yes / no
Software version:   ______________________   (photo taken: y/n)
Fob remote start:   yes / no
Date checked:       ______________________
```

## Recalls

- [ ] VIN run at `nhtsa.gov/recalls` — result: **open recalls / none**
- [ ] VIN checked in myChevrolet or Owner Center — result: **open items / none**
- [ ] Anything open is **scheduled or completed** at a dealer
- [ ] Confirmed no charge for recall work

## Phone projection

Do these with the car actually running, not on accessory power.

- [ ] Certified data cable in the car, and a **spare known-good cable** stored somewhere
- [ ] CarPlay **or** Android Auto launches automatically within ~30 seconds of plugging in
- [ ] Maps display and give turn-by-turn audio through the car speakers
- [ ] Music plays, and **steering wheel controls** skip tracks
- [ ] A test call connects — and **the far end can hear her clearly** (call someone and ask; this is
      the check people skip and it's the one that finds the bad microphone)
- [ ] Voice assistant responds from the steering wheel button
- [ ] Unplug mid-drive: audio falls back to Bluetooth or radio without the unit hanging

## Bluetooth

- [ ] Phone pairs and reconnects **automatically** on the next start
- [ ] Stale pairings from old phones and previous owners **deleted**
- [ ] Hands-free call works with the cable unplugged
- [ ] Contacts and recent calls appear on the car screen

## OnStar and the app

- [ ] myChevrolet installed, VIN added, **no prior-owner account blocking it**
- [ ] Account holder is **her**, with correct contact details
- [ ] Connected services **expiration date known** and written down above
- [ ] Fob remote start tested (if equipped) — engine actually starts
- [ ] Decision made on whether to pay for a plan after expiry, and why

## Basic function sweep

- [ ] Backup camera displays a clean image in reverse
- [ ] All speakers produce sound — balance and fade centered
- [ ] Climate controls respond
- [ ] Clock and date correct
- [ ] Radio presets set the way she wants them
- [ ] Screen brightness and day/night behavior sensible after dark

## Stability check

- [ ] Cold start after the car has sat overnight: radio boots normally
- [ ] No screen freezes, black screens, or spontaneous reboots across a full drive
- [ ] Boot time is unremarkable — not creeping longer over subsequent starts

If any stability box fails, go back to [Phase 6](06-troubleshooting.md), do the sleep reset, and
re-run this section before booking service.

## Hand it over

The genuinely useful things to tell Wendy, in plain terms:

1. **The car's own software isn't going to update, and that's normal.** The car gets better when her
   phone gets better. Keep the phone current.
2. **If the screen freezes:** turn the car off, open and close the door, **don't lock it**, wait 15
   minutes. That fixes it most of the time.
3. **Use the good cable, in the front USB port.** If projection acts up, swap to the spare cable
   before assuming anything is broken.
4. **Recalls are always free**, at any Chevy dealer, forever — worth re-running the VIN check once a
   year.
5. **The connected-services free term is expiring around now.** The app keeps the manual, service
   history, and recall lookup either way. Only the remote commands go away.
