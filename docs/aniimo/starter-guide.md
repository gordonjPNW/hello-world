# Aniimo — Xbox Starter Guide

A first-week guide for **Aniimo**, the free-to-play open-world creature-catching RPG, played on
Xbox Series X|S. Researched 2026-09-26, ten days after launch. Community guides still disagree on
some numbers, so treat exact percentages as approximate and trust what the game shows you.

## The basics

| | |
|---|---|
| **Released** | September 16, 2026 on Xbox Series X\|S, PS5 and PC; iOS/Android on September 23 |
| **Price** | Free to play. Pre-order packs and paid currency are optional |
| **Gacha?** | No. There are no character banners or pity counters. You get Aniimo by catching them, hatching eggs and completing events |
| **Crossplay** | Yes, with PC, PS5 and mobile, on the same server |
| **Cross-save** | Yes. Progress follows your developer account, but **only within one server**. Servers do not share progress |
| **Co-op** | Online, up to 4 players |

**Before your first login:** pick your server carefully. Choose the region you and your friends
will play on. Switching later means starting over on the new server.

## Step 1 — Pick your starter

You get one starter, **Helion** or **Lunara**. You can't get the other one later (trading isn't
in the game at launch), but it doesn't block completing your Aniilog (the creature log).

| | Helion | Lunara |
|---|---|---|
| Range | Melee (about 3 m) | Ranged (about 12 m), can attack while flying |
| Kit | Pull, stun, self-heal | Marks targets to lower their Light resistance; heals a little on marked hits |
| Best for | Players who like getting up close | Beginners and team play |

**Recommendation:** **Lunara** is the easier, more flexible pick. Pick **Helion** if you prefer
melee. Either one stays useful into the endgame, so level and gear your starter first and build
your team around it.

## Step 2 — Learn to catch

You catch Aniimo by throwing an **Aniipod**.

- **Sneak up from behind before you throw.** Hitting from behind raises your capture chance a lot
  (guides report about +150%). Often you can skip the fight completely.
- If they spot you, weaken them in battle first, then throw.
- Catch everything you see early on. Duplicates and new species both help while you work out
  what you like.

## Step 3 — Build a balanced team

Each Aniimo has one **role**:

| Role | Job |
|---|---|
| **DPS** | Deals the main damage |
| **Break** | Breaks the enemy's guard. A broken boss takes much more damage (about +250%), so this is your burst window |
| **Support** | Buffs the team or debuffs enemies |
| **Heal** | Restores HP |
| **Regen** | Keeps the team going with healing over time |

A safe beginner team: **1 DPS + 1 Break + 1 Heal or Regen + 1 flexible slot.** Swap between them
during a fight: bring in Break to crack a boss, then switch to DPS for the burst window.

### Elements

There are nine elements: **Light, Fire, Ice, Dark, Lightning, Grass, Water, Earth and Wind.**

- A super-effective hit does **1.6×** damage. A resisted hit does **0.625×**.
- Nothing is fully immune, so a bad matchup is weak, never useless.
- Attacking an Aniimo of the **same element** counts as resisted. The exception is Dark against
  Dark, which stays at normal damage.

Look up an enemy's element before a hard fight and bring an Aniimo that counters it.

## Step 4 — Use Twine

**Twine** links you with one of your Aniimo so you control it directly. It's used for getting
around, and in combat it's where most of your damage comes from, because being Twined unlocks the
bonuses in your skill tree. Use it all the time; it's not an optional extra.

## Step 5 — Follow the main story to raise your level cap

Your level is capped until your **Student Rank** goes up.

1. Follow the **Main Story** quest.
2. When it asks for a higher Student Rank, do the **Elite Training** quests.
3. Repeat. Hold off on hunting rare Aniimo until your cap is higher. You'll struggle to catch
   or beat them before then.

## Step 6 — Collect Lumin Ambers from the start

The shiny collectibles all over the world are **Lumin Ambers**.

- They're the **only way to increase your stamina**, and turning them in also unlocks new
  mechanics.
- They **don't apply by themselves**. Take them to the **Breezy Plains Branch** in the **Nimbus
  Fields** region to turn them in.
- Grab every one you pass and turn them in often.

## Currencies at a glance

The game has a lot of currencies. For beginners, only these three matter:

| Currency | Used for |
|---|---|
| **Starwish Coin** | The Starwish Gachapon (cosmetics and extras, not Aniimo) |
| **Voxel Coin** | Shops in Astra and the Super Lucky Star game |
| **Bud Ticket** | Furniture |

Since you catch your Aniimo rather than pull them, you **don't need to spend real money** to
progress. Put your early resources into your starter and your main team, not cosmetics.

## Xbox notes

- **Controls:** published guides only list the PC keyboard layout so far. Check the in-game
  settings menu for the controller layout, and find out which buttons *catch*, *Twine* and *swap
  Aniimo* before your first fight.
- **Playing with friends on other platforms:** make sure everyone is on the **same server**.
  Crossplay works across Xbox, PS5, PC and mobile.
- **Playing on your phone too:** log into the same developer account on the **same server**, and
  your progress carries over.

## Checklist for your first session

- [ ] Choose the server your friends will use
- [ ] Pick your starter (Lunara if unsure)
- [ ] Check the controller layout in the settings menu
- [ ] Catch a few Aniimo from behind with Aniipods
- [ ] Put together a DPS / Break / Heal team
- [ ] Try Twine in a fight
- [ ] Follow the Main Story to your first Elite Training
- [ ] Turn in any Lumin Ambers at the Breezy Plains Branch
