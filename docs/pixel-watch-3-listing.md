## 1. Before you list

### The one that matters: is it carrier-unlocked?

Verizon changed its unlock policy in **January 2026**. The old "automatically
unlocks after 60 days" rule is gone. A device now unlocks when it's **paid off
in full** — no time-based auto-unlock, and a buyer cannot inherit or accelerate
your eligibility.

So, before writing a price:

1. Log into My Verizon → Devices → check the watch for a remaining **device
   payment agreement balance**. Pay it off if there is one.
2. Confirm unlock status with Verizon support and get them to say it in writing
   (chat transcript / email). Screenshot it.
3. Remove the watch from your Verizon line / Number Share plan so it isn't
   still tied to your account.

**Locked vs. unlocked is the single biggest price lever.** If it's locked,
you're selling it honestly as a Verizon-locked unit and pricing lower — see §2.

Either way, it is still a fully functional Wi-Fi/Bluetooth smartwatch for any
buyer with an Android phone. Only the standalone-cellular feature depends on
carrier status. Say that in the listing; it saves the sale.

### The rest of the checklist

- [ ] **Factory reset** — your photos show the language-picker setup screen, so
      this looks done. Good; keep one of those photos, it's proof.
- [ ] **Remove from your Google account** so it isn't tied to Find My Device.
- [ ] **Do you have the charger?** The Pixel Watch 3 uses a proprietary magnetic
      puck. Without it the buyer has to source one, and it knocks $20–30 off.
      If you have it, photograph it — it's not in any of your current shots.
- [ ] **Box and papers?** Original packaging adds a bit and helps on Swappa.
- [ ] **Both band halves + any second band size?** Your flat-lay shows one
      complete band. If the box had a second (S and L), include both and say so.
- [ ] **Blur the serial number** engraved on the caseback before uploading
      photo #3. Publishing it invites warranty/IMEI fraud against you.

## 2. Pricing

Swappa's August 2026 data puts used Pixel Watch 3 prices starting around
**$181**, with unlocked black 45mm LTE listings sitting roughly **$150–$230**
depending on condition. Note those are *asking* prices, not sold prices.

| Scenario | Suggested ask |
|---|---|
| Unlocked, with charger, good cosmetic condition | **$170–$200** |
| Unlocked, no charger | **$145–$170** |
| Verizon-locked, with charger | **$120–$150** |
| Verizon-locked, no charger | **$100–$125** |

**Verify before you commit to a number.** Go to eBay, search
`Pixel Watch 3 45mm LTE`, and filter to **Sold Items** — that's real
transaction data, and it's the number that matters. Swappa's
[price page](https://swappa.com/prices/google-pixel-watch-3) is the other good
check.

Strategy: list at the top of your range with "or best offer" enabled. The
Pixel Watch 3 is two generations old now, so it moves on price, not patience.

## 7. Your photos

You have five shots. Here's how they grade out:

| Photo | Use it? | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Angled, setup screen visible | **Lead photo** | Screen on + factory-reset screen = instant credibility |
| Top-down, screen on | Yes | Clean, shows the full silhouette |
| Caseback close-up | Yes — **blur the serial first** | Shows the model engraving *and* the wear. Honest, and it prevents "not as described" claims |
| Second angled shot | Optional | Near-duplicate of #1; drop it if you're tight on slots |
| Flat lay, band separated | Yes | Nice clean composition, shows both band halves |

**Add these three shots:**

1. **The charging puck** next to the watch (or state clearly that it's not
   included — this is the #1 question you'll get).
2. **The front glass at an angle under a light** — buyers assume you're hiding
   screen scratches when there's no angled shot. If it's clean, this shot sells
   the watch.
3. **The watch on your wrist or on the charger showing a battery percentage.**
   Proves it holds a charge, which is the #2 question on any used smartwatch.

The marble countertop background is genuinely good — neutral, bright, no
clutter. Keep using it.

## 8. Questions you'll get (and the answers)

**"Is it unlocked?"** — Answer from your Verizon confirmation, never from a
guess. Getting this wrong is how you eat a return.

**"Does it work with iPhone?"** — No. Pixel Watch is Android-only. Say it in
the listing so you're not fielding it ten times.

**"How's the battery?"** — Pixel Watch 3 45mm is rated ~24h with always-on
display, up to 36h in battery saver. Say what you actually got.

**"Is the charger included?"** — Have the real answer ready and in the listing.

**"Will you take $X?"** — Decide your floor now, before the first offer.

## 9. Don't get scammed

- **Never ship before payment clears.** No Zelle, no Cash App, no "I'll send
  the shipping label."
- **Ship with tracking and require signature** above ~$150. It's your only
  defense in an "item not received" claim.
- **Photograph the packed box and the label** before you drop it off.
- **Local sales: meet at a police-department exchange zone**, cash only, count
  it before handing the watch over.
- **Ignore anyone who wants to move off-platform.** That's the entire scam,
  every time.
