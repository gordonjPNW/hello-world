# Phase 2 — Progression roadmap

**Time:** ongoing — this is the spine the other phases hang off
**You'll need:** Phase 1 done, especially your chosen campaign farmer

Account level, campaign progress, and champion rank all move together, and each one gates something
you want. This phase puts them in the right order so you stop wondering what to do next.

---

## The gates you're moving toward

| Level | What unlocks | Why you care |
|---|---|---|
| 30 | **Faction Wars** | Permanent stat bonuses per faction, and Sacred shards. Already open to you |
| 40 | **Doom Tower** | A second full reward track, plus its own gear and artifacts |
| 45–50 | **Hydra**, **Live Arena**, Advanced Great Hall | Endgame content. Hydra needs *twelve-plus* built champions, not five |
| 60 | Champion level cap at 6★ | The ceiling everything else is measured against |

Levelling is not something you do deliberately — it happens while you farm. But knowing what's
coming at 40 and 45 tells you when to start building roster *breadth* instead of depth. That
transition is the single biggest change in how you play, and Phase 7 is about preparing for it.

## Campaign: what to farm and where

Your farming stage should be **the highest-difficulty Chapter 12 stage your farmer clears reliably
on auto**. Work up to it rather than jumping — a stage you fail 10 % of the time on auto is worse
than an easier one you never fail, because auto-repeat stops on defeat and you lose the unattended
time.

| Stage type | What you're farming it for |
|---|---|
| **Chapter 12, stage 3** | XP density — the standard levelling farm |
| **Chapter 12, stage 6** | Silver and champion food |
| **Brimstone Path, stage 7** | Accessories, gated by campaign difficulty |

Difficulty ladder: **Normal → Hard → Brutal → Nightmare.** Brutal 12-3 is the classic early target
and where most accounts live for a long time. Don't push to Nightmare until Brutal runs clean.

> **Energy is your real currency.** Everything competes for it — campaign, dungeons, Doom Tower.
> Never let energy sit at cap; the refill timer stops. Dump the overflow into campaign runs even if
> you're mid-something-else.

## Champion food and what to feed away

Food is 1★–3★ champions used to level your keepers. Campaign generates it endlessly, so **never buy
food with anything scarcer than silver**.

Rules that hold up:

- **Feed 1★ and 2★ freely.** They exist to be food.
- **Rank up food before feeding it.** Levelling a 1★ to max and using it as fodder is far more
  efficient than feeding it raw — this is what the "food farming" loop is for.
- **Don't feed anything Rare or above until Phase 8.** Rares are fusion fodder, Faction Wars filler,
  and occasionally quietly good. Check before you sacrifice.
- **Keep one of every faction at 4★+** for Faction Wars, even mediocre ones.

## Ascension priority

Ascension (star rank) needs potions from the Potion Keeps, which rotate by day. Rank determines both
your level cap and your gear slots — **amulets and banners require a fully ascended champion**, so
ascension isn't optional flavour, it's the gate on your accessory slots.

Spend potions in this order:

1. **Your campaign farmer to 6★.** Everything else is slower until this is done.
2. **Your Clan Boss core** — the decrease-attack champion first (Phase 5).
3. **Your Dragon team damage dealer** (Phase 4).
4. **Breadth** — a healer, a Speed-aura champion, faction gaps.

**Do not spread ascension potions.** Three champions at 5★ is worse than one at 6★ in every
single piece of content you currently run.

> **Check the potion rotation before you plan.** Potion Keeps aren't all open every day, so an
> ascension you "need tomorrow" may actually be a three-day project. Look before you commit food to
> levelling someone you can't rank up.

## The build order, end to end

This is the whole early game in one list. Work down it; don't skip ahead.

1. **6★ campaign farmer, lifesteal gear, farming Brutal 12-3 on auto.**
2. **Clan Boss team that reliably takes chests** on the highest difficulty you can hold (Phase 5).
3. **Dragon 20 cleared on auto** — this is the gear gate, and the most important single milestone
   in the early game (Phase 4).
4. **Minotaur pushed as far as it goes**, for mastery scrolls (Phase 3).
5. **Spider for accessories**, Ice Golem for defensive gear (Phase 4).
6. **Arena climb for Great Hall medals** (Phase 6).
7. **Roster breadth** — Faction Wars, Doom Tower, and eventually Hydra (Phases 6–7).

Steps 1–3 are worth more than everything below them combined. If you only ever do three things,
do those.
