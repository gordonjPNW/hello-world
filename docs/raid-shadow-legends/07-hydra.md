# Phase 7 — Hydra

**Time:** read now (10 min), act later
**You'll need:** account level 45, and a clan

**This phase is not yet actionable for you, and padding it out would waste your time.** It's here so
that the work in Phases 4–6 points somewhere, because Hydra changes what "a good account" means and
the preparation starts long before you unlock it.

---

## What it is

A clan boss fight unlocked at **account level 45**, available only to clan members. You bring **six
champions** against **four Hydra heads at once**, and rewards scale with total damage — same
principle as the Demon Lord in Phase 5, different shape.

The rule that reframes your whole account:

> **Each champion can only be used in one Hydra battle per week.**

You get multiple attempts per week. Six champions per team, and no reuse, means a competitive Hydra
week needs **eighteen-plus genuinely built champions** — geared, ascended, mastered. Not eighteen
champions you own. Eighteen you'd be happy to field.

That's the shift. Everything before Hydra rewards concentration: one farmer, one Dragon team, one
Clan Boss team. Hydra rewards **depth**, and depth takes months to build. Knowing that now changes
what you do with your spare gear today.

## What that means for you right now

You don't build Hydra teams at level 40. You build the conditions for them:

| Do this now | Because |
|---|---|
| **Stop selling your second-best gear.** Keep decent 5★ pieces even when someone better is wearing them | Your eventual second and third Hydra teams wear exactly this |
| **Ascend broadly once your core is done** | Amulets and banners need full ascension. Eighteen champions is eighteen ascensions |
| **Say yes to Faction Wars breadth** (Phase 6) | The same bench serves both |
| **Bank shards for 2× events** (Phase 8) | Depth comes from volume of summons, which comes from event timing |
| **Join a clan that actually fights** | Hydra Clash needs ten clan members at level 45+ and sustained clan activity. A dead clan locks you out of content you've earned |

## The mechanics worth knowing early

- **Heads differ.** Each Hydra head punishes a different thing — one head's mechanic makes debuffs
  backfire, another rewards them. There is no single "good Hydra team," which is why depth beats
  quality here.
- **Damage is scored across the week**, so a team that survives long is worth more than a team that
  bursts and dies. The Phase 5 instinct transfers directly.
- **Block Debuffs, Cleanse, revives and Ally Protection** are all more valuable here than in
  dungeons. When you're deciding whether to keep a support champion with no obvious home — this is
  their home.

## When to come back to this

Come back when **all** of these are true:

- [ ] Account level 45+
- [ ] Dragon 20 auto-clearing (Phase 4)

Until then, Hydra is a reason to keep gear rather than a thing to do.
