# Phase 4 — Dungeon squads

**Time:** ongoing — this is where most of your energy goes for months
**You'll need:** Phase 3 read. Accuracy targets matter here

Six dungeons, four that drop things you need, one that only drops mastery scrolls, and one that
rotates. Dragon first, and it isn't close.

Fill in the **Your pick** column in each table from your Phase 1 champion list.

## Priority order

| Order | Dungeon | Target stage | Why this order |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dragon's Lair** | **20** | Gear, especially speed boots. Gates everything else |
| 2 | **Minotaur's Labyrinth** | as deep as you hold | Mastery scrolls, which multiply your Clan Boss damage |
| 3 | **Ice Golem's Peak** | 20 | Easiest of the four. Free defensive gear while you build up |
| 4 | **Spider's Den** | 15 → 20 | The only accessory source |
| 5 | **Fire Knight's Castle** | 10 → 20 | Hardest early. Needs a specific champion type you may not own yet |
| — | **Potion Keeps** | as needed | Run on the days the potion you need is open |

**Do not spread your energy evenly across these.** Push Dragon until it stalls, then spend the
stalled time elsewhere while your gear catches up.

## The universal dungeon team shape

Nearly every dungeon team is the same five jobs:

| Slot | Why it's there |
|---|---|
| **Decrease Defense or Weaken** | Multiplies your whole team's damage. The single most valuable dungeon debuff |
| **Damage dealer** | Converts that debuff into a dead boss |
| **Sustain** | Healer, shielder, or Ally Protection — keeps the run from ending |
| **Control** | Decrease Attack, Provoke, Freeze, or Stun — reduces incoming damage instead of healing it |
| **Utility / speed** | Increase Speed, Turn Meter control, or a second damage dealer |

Swap the emphasis per dungeon. The jobs don't change.

## Dragon's Lair — your priority

The Dragon has two adds and hits the whole team hard. You need to survive the AoE, strip his
defense, and kill him before attrition wins.

| Slot | Why it's there | Common early champions | Your pick |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decrease DEF | Boss has high defense; this is most of your damage | Warmaiden, Spirithost, Jizoh, Diabolist | |
| Damage dealer | Single-target burst on the boss | Kael, Coldheart, Executioner, your best-geared attacker | |
| Healer / sustain | His AoE will grind you down | Diabolist, Apothecary, Doompriest, any healer | |
| Decrease ATK | Cuts incoming damage roughly in half — better than out-healing it | Warmaiden variants, Apothecary-tier support | |
| Speed / second DPS | Increase Speed makes every run faster | Apothecary, Diabolist, Aothar | |

**Target: stage 20.** Progress in steps — clear 13 reliably, then 15, then 20. Auto-clearing 20 is
the milestone that unlocks the rest of your account.

> **When you stall on Dragon,** the answer is almost always Accuracy on the decrease-defense
> champion or more sustain — not more attack.

## Minotaur's Labyrinth — masteries

No gear here. You come for scrolls, and only one champion on the team gets them per clear, so bring
the champion whose masteries you're currently buying.

| Slot | Why it's there | Common early champions | Your pick |
|---|---|---|---|
| The champion you're farming scrolls for | Scrolls go to a team member — make sure it's the right one | your Clan Boss core | |
| Sustain | Long fight, chip damage | any healer | |
| Damage | Adds spawn and need clearing | AoE attacker | |
| Decrease DEF / Weaken | Speeds the boss down | as above | |
| Control | Reduces incoming pressure | Provoke / Decrease ATK | |

Push as deep as you reliably hold. Depth matters more than speed here — higher stages pay far
better scrolls, and you need 1,650 per champion.

## Ice Golem's Peak — the friendly one

The easiest of the four gear dungeons and a good confidence-builder while your Dragon team gears up.
Its adds apply control effects, so cleanse or debuff-blocking helps.

| Slot | Why it's there | Common early champions | Your pick |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decrease DEF | Same as everywhere | Warmaiden, Spirithost | |
| Damage dealer | Boss has a big health pool | your best attacker | |
| Healer / cleanser | Adds apply stuns and freezes | Apothecary, Doompriest | |
| AoE | Clears the adds | Kael, Elhain, Bellower | |
| Flex | Whatever your roster is deepest in | | |

## Spider's Den — accessories

The Spider summons spiderlings that must be cleared or they'll sustain her. AoE is mandatory, not
optional, and this is a dungeon where multi-hit and area damage both shine.

| Slot | Why it's there | Common early champions | Your pick |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AoE damage** | Spiderlings, constantly | Kael, Bellower, Elhain, Galek | |
| Decrease DEF | Boss damage | Warmaiden, Spirithost | |
| Healer | Sustained chip damage from the swarm | Apothecary, Diabolist | |
| Decrease ATK | Blunts the swarm's output | any decrease-ATK champion | |
| Single-target DPS | Finishes the boss once adds are controlled | Coldheart, Executioner | |

**Target: 15 first, then 20.** Rings drop earlier than amulets and banners are usable — remember
amulets and banners need a fully ascended champion (Phase 2).

## Fire Knight's Castle — the wall

Fire Knight carries a **shield that only breaks by taking a number of separate hits**. Raw damage
does not remove it. If you don't own multi-hit champions, this dungeon stays closed no matter how
well geared you are — and that's normal, not a mistake you made.

| Slot | Why it's there | Common early champions | Your pick |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Multi-hit champion** | Breaks the shield. Non-negotiable — bring two if you have them | any champion whose A1 hits 3+ times | |
| Second multi-hit | Halves the time the shield is up | | |
| Healer / sustain | His damage ramps the longer the fight runs | Apothecary, Doompriest | |
| Decrease DEF | Damage once the shield is down | Warmaiden, Spirithost | |
| Decrease ATK / control | Survivability | | |

**Target: clear 10 for the early rewards, then park it.** Come back for 20 when you own the right
champions. Grinding Fire Knight without multi-hitters is the most common early-account energy sink.

> **This is a roster gap, not a gear gap.** Add "multi-hit champion" to your Phase 8 event
> shopping list.

## Potion Keeps

Run these on the days the potion you need is open, and only as far as your ascension plan requires.
They're a means to Phase 2's ascension order, not a place to live.
