# Phase 5 — Clan Boss

**Time:** ~1 hour to set up, then daily
**You'll need:** an active clan, and Phase 3's mastery guidance

The Clan Boss is the best repeatable reward source available to an early account, and the biggest
single skill jump in the game. Rewards scale with **total damage dealt**, not with whether you win —
so a team that survives longer beats a team that hits harder and dies.

---

## How the rewards work

You spend your daily key allowance on the Demon Lord, and your accumulated damage places you in a
chest tier. Higher difficulties pay better chests, but only if you can survive them long enough to
out-damage the tier below. **Running Brutal badly pays worse than running Hard well.**

Difficulty ladder: **Easy → Normal → Hard → Brutal → Nightmare → Ultra-Nightmare.**

Sit at the hardest difficulty where your team survives most of the fight. Move up when you're
consistently topping out your current tier, not before.

> **The boss's affinity rotates.** Champions with the advantageous affinity land debuffs more
> reliably and take less punishment. If you have depth, swap your team to suit the week.

## The three team archetypes

| Archetype | What it needs | Where you are |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed team** | Nothing special — survivability, debuffs, and masteries | **Start here.** This is your team |
| **Counterattack** | Specific champions and reasonable gear | Mid-game |
| **Unkillable** | Precise speed tuning on 5★/6★ speed gear from Dragon 20+ | Later. See below |

Almost every guide you'll find online is about unkillable teams. Ignore them for now — they require
speed values you physically cannot reach on 4★ gear, and attempting one before Dragon 20 fails on
the speed gap alone regardless of how good the champions are.

## Your team — the early speed team

| Slot | Why it's there | Common early champions | Your pick |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Decrease Attack** | The most valuable debuff in the fight. Roughly halves incoming damage, which multiplies how long you survive and therefore your total damage | Check every Rare you own — it's common and easy to overlook | |
| **Poisoner** | Poison scales off the boss's enormous health pool and is your main early damage source. Stack multiple | Frozen Banshee, Kael | |
| **Healer / cleanser** | Keeps the team alive through the boss's AoE turns | Apothecary, Doompriest, Diabolist | |
| **Decrease DEF or Weaken** | Multiplies everyone's direct damage | Warmaiden, Spirithost, Diabolist | |
| **Damage dealer** | Converts the debuffs into damage | your best-geared attacker | |

**Apothecary deserves a specific mention** — his Increase Speed makes the whole team take more
turns, which is more total damage from every other slot. If you own him, he's rarely wrong here.

### What to gear them in

- **Accuracy first.** Clan Boss Resistance is high; a poison that doesn't land is zero damage. Aim
  for **200+ Accuracy** on debuffers at Brutal and above (Phase 3).
- **Then survivability** — HP and DEF, plus Lifesteal or Immortal on the champions taking hits.
- **Speed last, until you're tuning.** Before you have Dragon 20 gear, more speed just means dying
  sooner. Survive first.

### Masteries are not optional here

This is where Phase 3's mastery work pays off. **Warmaster or Giant Slayer on five champions is
worth more than any gear upgrade you can make right now** — frequently doubling total team damage.

Re-read the rule before spending scrolls: multi-hit champions take Giant Slayer, everyone else takes
Warmaster. Get it right the first time; 1,650 scrolls is weeks of Minotaur.

## The path forward

1. **Now:** speed team, five champions with Offense-tree masteries, sitting at the highest
   difficulty you survive.
2. **After Dragon 20:** you finally have 5★/6★ speed boots. Now speed tuning becomes possible.
3. **Then:** pick a published unkillable or counterattack composition that matches champions you
   actually own, and tune to it. This is a gear milestone, not a skill one — don't rush it.

> **Use every key, every day.** An unspent key is a chest you didn't get. This is the single most
> common cause of slow early accounts, and it costs nothing to fix.

## Done when

- [ ] In an active clan that actually fights the boss
- [ ] Five-champion team assembled with the roles above filled in
- [ ] Decrease Attack in the team, or on the event shopping list if you own none
- [ ] Debuffers at 200+ Accuracy for Brutal and above
- [ ] Warmaster / Giant Slayer bought for all five, chosen correctly per champion
- [ ] Parked at the hardest difficulty you *survive*, not the hardest you can enter
- [ ] Keys spent daily, without exception
