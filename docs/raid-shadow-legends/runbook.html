Where the progress actually is

1

One 6★ campaign farmer, in Lifesteal, before anything else (Phase 2). One well-built champion beats five half-built ones. This is the mistake that costs new accounts months.

2

Dragon 20, cleared on auto (Phase 4). The gear gate for the whole game. Every ambitious build — including every unkillable Clan Boss team — is downstream of the 5★ and 6★ speed boots that drop here.

3

Bank resources, then spend them during events (Phase 8). Identical work, wildly different rewards, decided entirely by when you do it.

01

Account audit

~30 minDo firstNo risk

Skipping this doesn't break anything. It just means every later decision — who to ascend, which dungeon to push, whether an event is worth chasing — gets made on a guess.

Record your account state

Inventory your champions

List everything at 4★ or above. For each, note the debuffs and buffs — Decrease ATK, Decrease DEF, Weaken, Poison, HP Burn, Increase SPD, Heal, Revive, Block Debuffs, Provoke — and whether they hit all enemies, one, or multiple times.

Easy to miss

Hit count matters more than it sounds. Multi-hit champions are the only answer to Fire Knight's shield, and AoE is what makes a campaign farmer.

Pick your campaign farmer

The most important early decision. A campaign farmer clears a stage on auto-repeat forever without dying, generating your XP, silver, and champion food.

RequirementWhy
Hits all enemies on A1Auto-battle leans on the default attack. Single-target farmers are slow
Lifesteal setSurvives with no healer, so fewer champions split the XP
One-shots wavesTwo turns per wave is half a farmer
SpeedDirectly multiplies runs per hour

Common picks: Kael, Elhain, Athel, Galek, Executioner, Warmaiden, Bellower. Commit to one — it gets your best gear, food, and potions first, ahead of anyone who looks better on a tier list.

Don't

Don't feed away Rares yet. Some are fusion fodder, some are Faction Wars filler, and some are quietly good. Food is cheaper from campaign than from regret.

Campaign: what to farm

Farm the highest-difficulty Chapter 12 stage your farmer clears reliably on auto. A stage you fail 10 % of the time is worse than an easier one you never fail — auto-repeat stops on defeat and you lose the unattended time.

StageFarm it for
Chapter 12-3XP density — the standard levelling farm
Chapter 12-6Silver and champion food
Brimstone Path 7Accessories, gated by campaign difficulty

Difficulty ladder: Normal → Hard → Brutal → Nightmare. Brutal 12-3 is the classic early target. Don't push to Nightmare until Brutal runs clean.

Energy

Energy is your real currency, and the refill timer stops at cap. Never let it sit full — dump the overflow into campaign even if you're mid-something-else.

Ascension priority

Rank determines your level cap and your gear slots — amulets and banners require a fully ascended champion. Spend potions in this order, one champion at a time:

  1. Campaign farmer to 6★. Everything is slower until this is done
  2. Clan Boss core — the Decrease Attack champion first
  3. Dragon team damage dealer
  4. Breadth — a healer, a Speed aura, faction gaps

Three champions at 5★ is worse than one at 6★ in every piece of content you currently run.

03

Gear, sets and masteries

~45 minDo first

Champion power comes overwhelmingly from gear, and gear power from substats on high-rarity pieces — not from set bonuses. New accounts get this backwards, chase set completion, and wonder why their 6★ still can't clear Dragon 15.

The three slots that carry your build

Weapon, helmet and shield have fixed main stats. Your decisions live in gauntlets (ATK/DEF/HP/C.RATE/C.DMG), chestplate (ATK/DEF/HP/ACC/RES) and boots (ATK/DEF/HP/SPD).

Keep these

Speed boots are the most valuable item in the game for an early account. Speed determines how often a champion acts, which multiplies everything else they do.

Upgrading rules that save you a fortune

Substats roll at +4, +8, +12 and +16, which is why strategy matters:

  1. Only upgrade 5★ and 6★ gear. Below that is silver, not equipment. Sell it
  2. Take a piece to +8 and reassess. If the rolls went nowhere useful, stop
  3. Push to +16 only on pieces that rolled well
  4. Never upgrade gear you're about to replace. Dragon 20 replaces all of it

Accuracy is not optional

A debuff that doesn't land does nothing. Enemy Resistance scales with stage, and Accuracy is what beats it.

ContentAccuracy target
Dungeon stages 10–15~100
Dungeon 20~150–180
Clan Boss, Brutal+200+
Doom Tower bosses250+

If a debuffer isn't landing, add Accuracy before you add anything else.

The universal team shape

Nearly every dungeon team is the same five jobs: Decrease DEF or Weaken, damage dealer, sustain, control (Decrease ATK, Provoke, Freeze), and utility. Swap the emphasis per dungeon; the jobs don't change.

Fire Knight — the wall

Fire Knight carries a shield that only breaks by taking a number of separate hits. Raw damage does not remove it.

Roster gap, not gear gap

If you don't own multi-hit champions, this dungeon stays closed no matter how well geared you are — and that's normal, not a mistake you made. Clear stage 10, park it, and add "multi-hit champion" to your Phase 8 shopping list. Grinding Fire Knight without one is the most common early-account energy sink.

Ice Golem and Minotaur

Ice Golem is the friendly one — same shape as Dragon, plus a cleanser for its adds' control effects. Minotaur drops no gear at all: bring the champion whose masteries you're currently buying, because scrolls go to a team member. Depth beats speed there — higher stages pay far better, and you need 1,650 per champion.

05

Clan Boss

~1 hrPriorityDaily

The best repeatable reward source available to an early account. Rewards scale with total damage dealt, not with winning — so a team that survives longer beats a team that hits harder and dies.

Pick your difficulty

Sit at the hardest difficulty where your team survives most of the fight. Running Brutal badly pays worse than running Hard well.

The three archetypes

ArchetypeWhat it needsWhere you are
Speed teamSurvivability, debuffs, masteriesStart here
CounterattackSpecific champions, reasonable gearMid-game
UnkillablePrecise speed tuning on 5★/6★ gear from Dragon 20+Later

Almost every guide online is about unkillable teams. Ignore them for now — they need speed values you physically cannot reach on 4★ gear, and attempting one before Dragon 20 fails on the speed gap alone regardless of champion quality.

Your team — the early speed team

SlotWhy it's thereCommon early picksYours
Decrease ATKThe most valuable debuff in the fight. Roughly halves incoming damage, which multiplies how long you survive and therefore your total damagecheck every Rare — it's common and easy to overlook___
PoisonerScales off the boss's enormous health pool. Your main early damage. Stack multipleFrozen Banshee, Kael___

Decrease DEF / WeakenMultiplies everyone's direct damageWarmaiden, Spirithost___

Apothecary deserves a specific mention — his Increase Speed gives the whole team more turns, which is more damage from every other slot.

Gear them in this order

  1. Accuracy. 200+ at Brutal and above. A poison that doesn't land is zero damage
  2. Survivability — HP, DEF, Lifesteal or Immortal on whoever takes hits
  3. Speed last, until you're actually tuning. Before Dragon 20 gear, more speed just means dying sooner
Biggest single upgrade available to you

Warmaster or Giant Slayer on all five champions is worth more than any gear upgrade you can make right now — frequently doubling total team damage. 1,650 scrolls each is weeks of Minotaur, so get the choice right the first time.

Every day

Use every key. An unspent key is a chest you didn't get — the single most common cause of slow early accounts, and it costs nothing to fix.

06

Arena, Doom Tower and Faction Wars

~1 hrLevel 40+ for DT

Three modes that share a theme: they pay in permanent account-wide power rather than gear. Great Hall and Faction Guardian bonuses apply to every champion you will ever own — which makes them worth starting badly rather than starting late.

Classic Arena — you're here for the medals

Arena is decided almost entirely on turn one. Whoever moves first and lands a big AoE usually wins before the other team acts.

SlotWhy it's thereCommon early picksYours
Speed aura leaderDecides who moves first. Put your highest Speed aura in the leader slotcheck your whole roster___
Turn meter / Increase SPDGuarantees you act before they doApothecary, Diabolist___
AoE nukerWins the game on turn oneKael, Bellower, Elhain, Galek___
Damage / controlCleans up, or Decrease ATK if you're losing the raceyour best attacker___

Gear these four in Speed above everything else. Arena is the one place where speed genuinely beats stats.

The Great Hall

Arena medals are the only currency that upgrades the Great Hall — permanent stat bonuses per affinity, applied to every champion you own.

Great Hall levelNeeds
1–3Bronze, Silver or Gold medals
4–6Silver or Gold only
7–10Gold only

Your Arena tier caps how far the Great Hall can go, so climbing isn't vanity. Spend early medals on HP and DEF — survivability compounds into longer Clan Boss fights, deeper dungeons, and more Faction Wars clears.

Doom Tower — from level 40

What it asks for that dungeons don't: Block Debuffs and Cleanse (several bosses win by stacking debuffs), roster breadth, and a reviver. Push until you stall, take the rewards, then spend the rest of your energy on Dragon. Fragments accumulate across rotations.

Faction Wars — already open to you

Each faction crypt needs five champions of that faction, which is exactly the point — it's the game's mechanism for making you build breadth. Faction Guardian bonuses are permanent, completing all crypts awards a Legendary, and it converts the bench you were going to feed away into progress.

Approach

Pick the two or three factions where you're deepest and chip at those crypts with what you have. Don't build champions for Faction Wars yet — gear rotates over easily and the crypts have no deadline.

Done when
07

Hydra

10 min readLevel 45+

Not yet actionable for you — it's here so the work in Phases 4–6 points somewhere. You bring six champions against four Hydra heads at once, and rewards scale with total damage.

The rule that reframes your account

Each champion can only be used in one Hydra battle per week. Six per team, no reuse, multiple attempts — a competitive week needs eighteen-plus genuinely built champions. Not owned. Built.

That's the shift. Everything before Hydra rewards concentration — one farmer, one Dragon team, one Clan Boss team. Hydra rewards depth, and depth takes months. Which changes what you do with spare gear today.

What to do now

Do this nowBecause
Stop selling your second-best 5★ gearYour eventual second and third Hydra teams wear exactly this
Ascend broadly once your core is doneAmulets and banners need full ascension. Eighteen champions is eighteen ascensions
Say yes to Faction Wars breadthThe same bench serves both
Bank shards for 2× eventsDepth comes from summon volume, which comes from event timing
Join a clan that actually fightsHydra Clash needs ten members at level 45+ and sustained clan activity

Heads differ — one punishes debuffs, another rewards them — so there is no single "good Hydra team," which is precisely why depth beats quality here. Block Debuffs, Cleanse, revives and Ally Protection are all worth more than in dungeons. When you're wondering whether to keep a support champion with no obvious home: this is their home.

08

Events, fusions and tournaments

~45 minHigh value

The highest-value habit in the game: do what you were going to do anyway, but at the moment it also scores event points. Farm 500 energy of Dragon during a Dungeon Divers tournament, or the week before — identical work, wildly different rewards.

What to bank for what

EventScores points forSo bank this
Champion TrainingLevelling and ascendingUn-levelled food, potions, silver
Dungeon DiversDungeon runs and clearsEnergy, and a dungeon you're mid-push on
Summon Rush / Champion ChaseSummoningShards. All of them
Artifact EnhancementUpgrading gearUnenhanced 5★/6★ gear, silver
Clan vs ClanEverything above, clan-wideEverything above

Nearly all of them start at 08:00 UTC. Convert that to your local time once and remember it — the in-game calendar often shows only a few days ahead, which is how people get caught with an empty bank when a fusion drops.

The banking box

Keep a permanent stash you deliberately don't spend outside events: shards (never pull outside a summon event), champion food, ascension potions, unenhanced 5★/6★ gear, silver, and energy at cap the night before a start. The discipline is the difficulty — holding a shard for three weeks feels bad and is correct.

Fusions — how they actually work

A fusion gives you a champion free if you complete overlapping events inside a fixed window. Traditional fusions want you to summon and sacrifice specific champions upward; fragment fusions want placement in events and tournaments, and are generally the better free-to-play target.

The pattern never changes: the fusion demands you place well in several events at once. Which is why the banking box exists. A fusion is not won during the fusion — it's won in the three weeks before it, while you sit on resources.

Before committing, ask

  • Can I realistically hit every requirement? Partial completion earns nothing
  • Do I want the champion, or do I want to have won? Check it against your gap list
  • What does it cost me? A fusion abandoned at 60 % has burned weeks of banked resources

It is correct to skip fusions. An account that skips two and fully banks for the third ends up ahead of one that half-chases all three.

Where to check what's running

Schedules rotate monthly, so this runbook deliberately lists no dates — they'd be wrong within weeks. Check live: the HellHades fragment fusion tracker, the AyumiLove fusion guide, and the in-game calendar.

Your event shopping list

When an event offers a choice, check it against your gaps rather than a tier list. A champion that opens content you can't currently run is worth more than a stronger one duplicating a role you've filled.

  • Decrease Attack — if Phase 5 left that slot empty
  • Multi-hit — opens Fire Knight
  • Block Debuffs / Cleanse — opens Doom Tower
  • Speed aura leader — Arena, and therefore the Great Hall
  • Depth in thin factions — Faction Wars, and eventually Hydra

09

Routine and validation

~10 min dailyEvery day

The daily loop

Ten minutes, in this order — the timed things go first so they run while you do the rest.

  1. Spend Clan Boss keys. Every one, every day
  2. Claim daily login and quests
  3. Start a campaign auto-run if energy is near cap, so it drains meanwhile
  4. Arena battles — free attempts, for medals
  5. Dungeon push — usually Dragon
  6. Minotaur run for whoever's masteries you're buying
  7. Check the calendar for anything starting at 08:00 UTC soon
  8. Bank, don't spend

Weekly: clan quests, Doom Tower rotation, a Faction Wars crypt attempt or two, Potion Keeps for the next ascension, and a check that your farming stage is still the hardest one you clear cleanly.

Has this worked?

Check monthly against your Phase 1 notes. Numbers, not vibes.

If only one thing

If Dragon 20 isn't moving, nothing else matters. Check the two usual causes in order: Accuracy on the decrease-defense champion, then sustain. It is almost never a lack of attack.

Signals you've drifted

  • Energy sitting at cap — you've stopped generating, and the refill timer stopped too
  • Unspent Clan Boss keys — the most expensive habit in the game
  • Six champions at 5★ and none at 6★ — you're spreading. Pick one and finish
  • Fully upgraded 4★ gear — silver you can't get back
  • Chasing a fusion you can't finish — better to skip and bank
  • Pulling shards the day you get them — halves their value
