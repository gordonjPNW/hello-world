# Phase 8 — Events, fusions & tournaments

**Time:** ~45 minutes to set up your banking habit, then it runs itself
**You'll need:** Phases 2–5 running. This phase converts routine play into free champions

The single highest-value habit in RAID: **do the things you were going to do anyway, but at the
moment they also score event points.** An account that farms 500 energy of Dragon during a Dungeon
Divers tournament and one that farms the same 500 energy the week before have done identical work
and received wildly different rewards.

That's the whole phase. Everything below is mechanics for making it happen.

---

## The event types you'll see

| Event | Scores points for | So bank this |
|---|---|---|
| **Champion Training** | Levelling and ascending champions | Un-levelled food, ascension potions, silver |
| **Dungeon Divers** | Dungeon runs and clears | Energy, and a dungeon you're mid-push on |
| **Summon Rush / Champion Chase** | Summoning champions | **Shards.** All of them |
| **Artifact Enhancement** | Upgrading gear | Unenhanced 5★/6★ gear, and silver |
| **Advanced Quests** | Mixed objectives across modes | General readiness |
| **Clan vs Clan (CvC)** | Everything above, clan-wide | Everything above |
| **Arena tournaments** | Arena wins | Unspent arena refills |

**Nearly all of them start at 08:00 UTC.** Convert that to your local time once and remember it —
the in-game calendar often only shows a few days ahead, which is why people get caught with an empty
bank when a fusion drops.

## The banking box

Keep a permanent stash you deliberately do not spend outside events:

- **Shards** — never pull outside a summon event or a 2× summon promotion. This is the biggest one
- **Champion food** — farmed but not fed. Levelled-up fodder scores more than raw fodder
- **Ascension potions** — held for Champion Training
- **Unenhanced 5★/6★ gear** — for Artifact Enhancement
- **Silver** — the currency that turns the bank into points. Chronically short on every account
- **Energy** — let it sit at cap the night before an event starts, then dump it

> **The discipline is the difficulty.** Every one of these is something you want to spend
> immediately. Holding a shard for three weeks feels bad and is correct.

## Fusions — how they actually work

A fusion gives you a champion for free if you complete a set of overlapping events and tournaments
inside a fixed window. Two shapes:

- **Traditional fusion.** Summon or acquire a set of specific champions, sacrifice them upward
  (Rares → Epics → the Legendary). Requires deep resources and usually shards.
- **Fragment fusion.** Collect fragments by placing in specific events and tournaments. More
  achievable free-to-play, and generally the better target for an early account.

The pattern that never changes: **the fusion demands you place well in several events at once.**
That's why the banking box exists. A fusion is not won during the fusion — it's won in the three
weeks before it, while you sit on resources.

### Should you chase this one?

Ask before committing, not halfway through:

1. **Can I realistically hit every requirement?** Fusions requiring deep Dungeon Divers placement
   are gated on dungeon stages you may not clear yet. Partial completion earns you nothing.
2. **Do I want the champion, or do I want to have won?** Check what the fusion champion actually
   does against your Phase 1 gap list.
3. **What does it cost me?** A fusion you abandon at 60 % has burned weeks of banked resources.

**It is correct to skip fusions.** An early account that skips two and fully banks for the third
ends up further ahead than one that half-chases all three.

## Where to check what's running

Fusion schedules and event calendars rotate monthly, so this guide deliberately doesn't list dates —
they'd be wrong within weeks. Check live:

- [HellHades fragment fusion tracker](https://hellhades.com/raid/fragment-fusion-tracker/) — what's
  running now and what it requires
- [AyumiLove fusion guide](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-fusion-guide/) —
  requirements broken down per fusion
- The in-game calendar — authoritative, but only shows a few days ahead

## Your event shopping list

From Phase 1's gap list and Phase 4's Fire Knight note, you're looking for:

- [ ] A **Decrease Attack** champion (if Phase 5 left this empty)
- [ ] A **multi-hit** champion (opens Fire Knight)
- [ ] A **Block Debuffs / Cleanse** champion (opens Doom Tower)
- [ ] A **Speed aura** leader (Arena, and therefore Great Hall)
- [ ] Depth in your thin factions (Faction Wars, and eventually Hydra)

When an event offers a choice of reward, or you're deciding whether a fusion is worth chasing, check
it against this list rather than a tier list. **A champion that opens content you can't currently
run is worth more than a stronger one that duplicates a role you've already filled.**
