# Phase 3 — Gear, sets & masteries

**Time:** ~45 minutes to read and re-sort your inventory
**You'll need:** Phase 2 running — a farmer generating silver

Champion power in RAID comes overwhelmingly from gear, and gear power comes overwhelmingly from
**substats on high-rarity pieces**, not from set bonuses. New accounts get this backwards, chase set
completion, and wonder why their 6★ champion still can't clear Dragon 15.

---

## The nine slots, and which ones you actually choose

| Slot | Main stat | Your decision? |
|---|---|---|
| Weapon | Always flat ATK | No |
| Helmet | Always flat HP | No |
| Shield | Always flat DEF | No |
| **Gauntlets** | ATK / DEF / HP / C.RATE / C.DMG | **Yes** |
| **Chestplate** | ATK / DEF / HP / ACC / RES | **Yes** |
| **Boots** | ATK / DEF / HP / **SPD** | **Yes** |
| Ring | ATK / DEF / HP | Minor |
| Amulet | ATK / DEF / HP / C.DMG | Minor |
| Banner | ATK / DEF / HP / ACC / RES | Minor |

Three slots carry your build: **gauntlets, chestplate, boots**. Everything else is substats with a
fixed main stat attached.

> **Speed boots are the most valuable item in the game for an early account.** A champion's speed
> determines how often they act, which multiplies everything else they do. When you're deciding what
> to keep, speed boots win.

## Upgrading rules that save you a fortune

Substats roll at **+4, +8, +12, and +16**. This is why upgrade strategy matters:

1. **Only upgrade 5★ and 6★ gear.** 4★ and below is silver, not equipment. Sell it.
2. **Take a piece to +8 and reassess.** If neither roll went somewhere useful, stop and move on —
   the sunk silver is gone either way.
3. **Push to +16 only on pieces that rolled well.** A +16 with three dead substats is worse than a
   +8 you didn't waste 200k silver on.
4. **Never upgrade gear you're about to replace.** Everything you own now gets replaced by Dragon 20
   drops. Gear the team well enough to clear Dragon, then invest properly.

## Sets worth knowing early

You will not complete most sets, and that's fine — a two-piece Speed set on good substats beats a
six-piece set of junk.

| Set | What it does | Use it on |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed** | +12 % SPD | Almost everyone. The default correct answer |
| **Lifesteal** | Heals for a share of damage dealt | Your campaign farmer, above all else |
| **Life / Defense** | +15 % HP / +15 % DEF | Tanks, Clan Boss survivors |
| **Accuracy** | +40 ACC | Debuffers who miss (see below) |
| **Offense / Cruel / Savage** | Damage and crit | Dungeon damage dealers |
| **Immortal / Regeneration** | Sustain | Clan Boss and Doom Tower survivability |
| **Stun / Retaliation** | Chance-based extra effects | Arena and Faction Wars filler |

### Accuracy is not optional

A debuff that doesn't land does nothing. Enemy Resistance scales with dungeon stage and difficulty,
and your Accuracy stat is what beats it. A decrease-attack champion with 80 Accuracy in Dragon 20 is
a passenger.

Rough targets to aim at as you gear debuffers:

| Content | Accuracy to aim for |
|---|---|
| Early dungeon stages (10–15) | ~100 |
| Dungeon 20 | ~150–180 |
| Clan Boss, Brutal and up | ~200+ |
| Doom Tower bosses | 250+ |

If a debuffer isn't landing, add Accuracy before you add anything else.

## Where gear comes from

| Dungeon | What you go there for |
|---|---|
| **Dragon's Lair** | Your main gear farm — Speed, Lifesteal, Offense, Accuracy, Destroy. **Speed boots live here** |
| **Ice Golem's Peak** | HP and Defense sets. The easiest of the four, and a fine first push |
| **Fire Knight's Castle** | Offense, crit, Shield and Stun sets. Hard early — his shield needs multi-hit champions |
| **Spider's Den** | **The only dungeon that drops accessories** — rings, amulets, banners |
| **Minotaur's Labyrinth** | **Mastery scrolls only.** No gear at all |
| **Potion Keeps** | Ascension potions, rotating by day |

**Dragon 20 is the milestone.** It's where 5★ and 6★ gear becomes routine instead of lucky, and it
gates every ambitious build in the game — including the unkillable Clan Boss teams in Phase 5, which
simply cannot be speed-tuned on 4★ gear. Phase 4 is largely about getting there.

## Masteries

Masteries are permanent per-champion bonuses bought with scrolls from **Minotaur's Labyrinth**, the
only source. A full mastery set costs **1,650 scrolls per champion** — 100 basic, 600 advanced, 950
divine — so this is a slow, deliberate investment.

**Do your Clan Boss team first.** Masteries are worth more there than anywhere else in the early
game, often doubling a champion's damage contribution.

### Warmaster or Giant Slayer — pick one

Both live at the bottom of the Offense tree and you can only have one. The rule:

| Champion type | Take |
|---|---|
| Hits **once or twice** per turn, or AoE | **Warmaster** |
| Hits **three or more times** per turn (multi-hit) | **Giant Slayer** |

Giant Slayer procs per hit and scales off enemy max HP, which is why multi-hit champions want it
against huge health pools like the Clan Boss. Warmaster procs less often but hits harder per proc.
Get this wrong and you've spent 1,650 scrolls on the weaker option.

**Support champions** — healers, pure debuffers who deal no meaningful damage — take the Support
tree instead, prioritising the debuff-duration and Accuracy nodes.
