# Phase 1 — Account audit

**Time:** ~30 minutes
**Risk:** none — you're only writing things down
**You'll need:** somewhere to keep notes you'll come back to

Skipping this phase doesn't break anything. It just means every later decision — who to 6★, which
dungeon to push, whether an event is worth chasing — gets made on a guess. Half an hour now saves
weeks of misplaced resources.

---

## 1. Record your account state

| What | Where to find it | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Account level** | Top-left of the main screen | Gates Doom Tower (40), Live Arena & Hydra (45–50). Also sets your energy cap and refill rate |
| **Campaign progress** | Campaign map — highest chapter and difficulty cleared | Determines your farming stage and your XP rate |
| **Highest dungeon stage** per dungeon | Each dungeon's stage list | This is your real power level. Be honest — the highest stage you clear *reliably on auto*, not the one you scraped once |
| **Arena tier** | Arena → Classic | Gates Great Hall upgrades (Phase 6) |
| **Clan** | Clan tab | No clan means no Clan Boss chests and no Hydra. Fix this today if it's empty |

## 2. Inventory your champions

List every champion at 4★ or above, plus any rare-or-better you've been saving. For each:

- **Name, faction, affinity, rarity, current stars and level**
- **Their useful debuffs or buffs** — read the skill descriptions, not the tier list. The words that
  matter most early: *Decrease ATK, Decrease DEF, Weaken, Poison, HP Burn, Increase SPD, Ally
  Protection, Heal, Revive, Block Debuffs, Provoke*
- **Whether they hit all enemies, one, or multiple times**

That last one is easy to skip and matters more than it sounds. Multi-hit champions are the answer to
Fire Knight's shield and Spider's spiderlings, and AoE is what makes a campaign farmer.

> **Faction matters more than you'd think.** Faction Wars needs *five champions from the same
> faction*, and it's already unlocked for you. When two champions are otherwise equal, keep the one
> from a faction where you're thin.

## 4. Pick your campaign farmer

This is the single most important decision in the early game, and it's the reason this phase exists.

A campaign farmer clears a campaign stage on auto-repeat, indefinitely, without dying. It generates
your XP, your silver, and your champion food. Until you have one, everything else is slow.

**What the job needs:**

| Requirement | Why |
|---|---|
| **Hits all enemies** on their A1 | The default attack is what auto-battle uses most. Single-target farmers are slow |
| **Lifesteal set** (or a strong self-heal) | Survives indefinitely with no healer, so you take fewer champions and each gets more XP |
| **High enough damage to one-shot waves** | A farmer that takes two turns per wave is half a farmer |
| **Speed** | Directly multiplies your runs per hour |

**Common early picks:** Kael, Elhain, Athel, Galek (any starter works — Kael is the usual
recommendation and remains genuinely good), Executioner, Warmaiden, Bellower, Coldheart for
single-target-heavy stages.

**Your pick:** _______________

Commit to one. This champion gets your best gear, your food, and your ascension potions first,
ahead of anyone who looks better on a tier list. A 6★ farmer in mediocre gear beats a 5★
"better" champion in nothing.

> **Don't feed away your duplicates until Phase 2.** Some of them are Faction Wars filler, some are
> fusion fodder, and food is cheaper from campaign than from regret.

## 5. Note your gaps

Look at your list and write down which of these you *don't* have. These are the roles that block
content later, and knowing the gap tells you what to look for in an event:

- [ ] Decrease Attack (blocks a comfortable Clan Boss)
- [ ] Decrease Defense or Weaken (blocks dungeon damage)
- [ ] A healer or reviver (blocks Doom Tower and Faction Wars)
- [ ] Increase Speed (blocks fast everything)
- [ ] A multi-hit champion (blocks Fire Knight)
- [ ] Block Debuffs / Cleanse (blocks Doom Tower bosses)
- [ ] A Speed aura, ideally faction-wide (blocks Arena)
