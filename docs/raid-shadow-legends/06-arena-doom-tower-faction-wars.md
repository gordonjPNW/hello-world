# Phase 6 — Arena, Doom Tower & Faction Wars

**Time:** ~1 hour to set up, then a few minutes daily
**You'll need:** Phases 4–5 running. Level 40 for Doom Tower

Three modes that share a theme: **they pay in permanent account-wide power rather than gear.** Great
Hall bonuses and Faction Guardian bonuses apply to every champion you will ever own, forever. That
makes them worth more than their immediate rewards suggest, and worth starting badly rather than
starting late.

---

## Classic Arena — you're here for the medals

Arena is 4-vs-4 and it is decided almost entirely on **turn one**. Whoever moves first and lands a
big AoE usually wins before the other team acts. That makes speed the whole game.

The reason to care isn't the arena rewards — it's **medals**, which are the only currency that
upgrades the Great Hall.

| Slot | Why it's there | Common early champions | Your pick |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Speed aura leader** | A faction-wide or all-champion Speed aura decides who moves first. Put your highest Speed aura in the leader slot | any champion with a Speed aura — check your whole roster | |
| **Turn-meter / Increase Speed** | Guarantees you act before they do | Apothecary, Diabolist, Aothar | |
| **AoE nuker** | Wins the game on turn one | Kael, Bellower, Elhain, Galek | |
| **Second damage / control** | Cleans up, or Decrease ATK if you're losing the race | your best attacker | |

**Gear these four in Speed above everything else.** Arena is the one place where speed genuinely
beats stats — a fast, fragile team that acts first beats a tanky one that doesn't.

### The Great Hall

Medals from Arena wins buy Great Hall levels: permanent stat bonuses applied per affinity, to every
champion of that affinity you own.

- **Levels 1–3** can be bought with Bronze, Silver, or Gold medals
- **Levels 4–6** need Silver or Gold
- **Levels 7–10** need Gold only
- The **Advanced Great Hall** (Area Bonuses) opens around level 50

So your Arena tier caps how far the Great Hall can go. Climbing tiers isn't vanity — it's the
gate on a permanent power source.

**Spend early medals on HP and DEF** across affinities. Survivability compounds into everything:
longer Clan Boss fights, deeper dungeon stages, more Faction Wars clears. Attack and crit bonuses
are better later, when your champions are already surviving.

## Doom Tower — from level 40

A separate 120-floor tower with bosses every ten floors, rotating each cycle. Don't expect to get
deep on an early account; that's fine, the early floors still pay.

What Doom Tower asks for that dungeons don't:

- **Block Debuffs and Cleanse.** Several bosses win by stacking debuffs on you. Raw stats don't
  answer that; the right skill does.
- **Roster breadth.** Different floors demand different answers, and the secret rooms have specific
  affinity requirements.
- **A reviver.** Failing at floor 60 with one champion left is common, and a revive turns that loss
  into a clear.

Approach: **push until you stall, then stop.** Each rotation, climb to your wall, take the rewards,
and spend the rest of your energy on Dragon. The tower isn't going anywhere, and Doom Tower
fragments accumulate across rotations.

## Faction Wars — already open to you

Unlocked at level 30. Each faction has its own crypt, and you need **five champions of that faction**
to enter — which is precisely the point. It's the game's mechanism for making you build breadth
rather than perfecting one team.

Why it's worth doing badly:

- **Faction Guardian bonuses** are permanent stat increases to every champion of that faction, and
  they unlock from crypt progress. Like the Great Hall, they never stop paying.
- It converts your bench — the 4★ champions you were going to feed away — into progress.
- Completing all of the crypts awards a Legendary champion.

**How to approach it early:** pick the two or three factions where you're deepest, and chip at those
crypts with whatever you have. Do not build champions *for* Faction Wars yet. Gear rotates over from
other teams easily, and the crypts are permanent content with no deadline.

> **This is why Phase 1 told you not to feed away Rares.** A mediocre Rare from a thin faction is
> worth more sitting in a Faction Wars team than as a level or two on someone else.
