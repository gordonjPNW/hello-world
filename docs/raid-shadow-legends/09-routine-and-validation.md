# Phase 9 — Routine & validation

**Time:** ~20 minutes to set up, ~10 minutes a day to run
**You'll need:** Phases 1–8 read

Two things here: the daily loop that keeps the account compounding, and the checkpoints that tell
you whether any of this actually worked. The second matters — RAID is very good at making busywork
feel like progress.

---

## The daily loop

Ten minutes, in this order. The order matters: the timed things go first so they're running while
you do everything else.

1. **Spend Clan Boss keys.** Every one, every day. An unspent key is a chest you didn't get
2. **Claim daily login and quests** — free energy, silver, and occasionally shards
3. **Start a campaign auto-run** if your energy is near cap, so it drains while you do the rest
4. **Arena battles** — take your free attempts for medals (Phase 6)
5. **Dungeon push** — whatever Phase 4 says is current. Usually Dragon
6. **Minotaur run** for the champion whose masteries you're buying
7. **Check the calendar** for anything starting at 08:00 UTC in the next few days
8. **Bank, don't spend** — shards, potions, and unenhanced gear go in the box (Phase 8)

### Weekly

- Clan quests and clan activity — keeps your clan eligible for the content you want later
- Doom Tower progress, if it's a rotation you can push (level 40+)
- A Faction Wars crypt attempt or two
- Potion Keeps for whatever ascension is next in the queue
- Review: is your farming stage still the hardest one you clear cleanly?

## Validation — has this worked?

Check these monthly against what you wrote down in Phase 1. Numbers, not vibes.

| Checkpoint | Early target | What it proves |
|---|---|---|
| **Campaign farmer** | 6★, Lifesteal, auto-farming Brutal 12-3 without dying | Phase 2 done. Your resource engine runs unattended |
| **Dragon's Lair** | Stage 20, cleared on **auto**, reliably | The gear gate is open. This is the big one |
| **Clan Boss** | Taking chests daily on Brutal or better | Phase 5 done. Your best repeatable income |
| **Masteries** | Warmaster / Giant Slayer on all five Clan Boss champions | Roughly doubled Clan Boss damage |
| **Accuracy** | Debuffers at 200+ | Debuffs land, so your damage is real |
| **Great Hall** | HP and DEF bought across affinities | Permanent, account-wide, never stops paying |
| **Faction Wars** | Two or three crypts making progress | Roster breadth started before Hydra needs it |
| **Shards** | A bank you didn't pull | Phase 8 discipline is holding |

**If Dragon 20 isn't moving, nothing else matters.** Go back to Phase 4 and check the two usual
causes in order: Accuracy on the decrease-defense champion, then sustain. It is almost never a lack
of attack.

## Signals you've drifted

- **Energy sitting at cap.** You've stopped generating. Fix immediately — the refill timer stops too
- **Unspent Clan Boss keys.** The most expensive habit in the game
- **Six champions at 5★ and none at 6★.** You're spreading. Pick one and finish
- **Fully upgraded 4★ gear.** Silver you can't get back (Phase 3)
- **Chasing a fusion you can't finish.** Better to skip and bank (Phase 8)
- **Pulling shards the day you get them.** Halves their value

## Re-audit every month

Repeat Phase 1's inventory monthly. It takes fifteen minutes and it catches the two things you can't
see from inside the daily loop: roles you've now filled without noticing (so you can stop hunting
for them in events), and champions you've been levelling out of habit rather than plan.

## Done when

- [ ] Daily loop running without you thinking about it
- [ ] Weekly items on a routine
- [ ] Validation table checked against your Phase 1 notes
- [ ] Dragon 20 cleared on auto — or you know exactly which of the two causes is blocking you
- [ ] Monthly re-audit scheduled
